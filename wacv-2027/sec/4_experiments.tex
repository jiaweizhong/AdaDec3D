\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:experiments}

\paragraph{Dataset and split.}
We use the 13-organ BTCV/Synapse abdominal CT task with 18 training and 12
validation volumes.  The evaluated organs are spleen, kidneys, gallbladder,
esophagus, liver, stomach, aorta, inferior vena cava, portal/splenic veins,
pancreas, and adrenal glands.  Images are intensity-windowed and resampled
according to the public EffiDec3D training pipeline; training uses $96^3$
patches.  The same validation subjects are paired across all model comparisons.

\paragraph{Models.}
\fullmodel{} is the public full 3D UX-Net configuration with feature widths
$[48,96,192,384]$.  \effimodel{} uses the corresponding EffiDec3D decoder with
48 channels, additive skip aggregation, and resolution factor two.  Both are
trained for 45k iterations with Dice--cross-entropy loss and AdamW.  We evaluate
one \fullmodel{} run and two independently initialized \effimodel{} runs.

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
Segmentation quality is reported using mean and per-organ Dice and 95th
percentile Hausdorff distance (HD95).  Efficiency measurements include
parameters, MACs, latency, peak inference memory, and training time.
Sliding-window inference uses a $96^3$ region, batch size four, overlap 0.7,
and constant blending.  Observation confidence intervals resample subjects.

\paragraph{Reimplementation status.}
Our data were reconstructed from the public TransUNet/Synapse release, whose
NIfTI files have identity affine metadata, whereas the EffiDec3D paper refers
to a processed \texttt{btcv\_trns} dataset.  Consequently, we present the
experiment as a \emph{controlled reimplementation}, not an exact reproduction.
All comparisons within our study use the same local data and evaluation code.
We report the discrepancy from published numbers in full.
