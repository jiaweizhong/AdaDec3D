\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Efficient decoders for 3D segmentation.}
U-shaped encoder--decoder networks dominate volumetric segmentation.  Recent
backbones raise accuracy with heavier computation: UNETR~\cite{hatamizadeh2022unetr}
and Swin UNETR~\cite{tang2022swinunetr,he2023swinunetrv2} use transformer
encoders, while 3D UX-Net~\cite{lee20233duxnet} and
MedNeXt~\cite{roy2023mednext} use large-kernel convolutions, and
nnU-Net~\cite{isensee2021nnunet} shows that pipeline configuration alone is
strongly competitive.  In several of these models the decoder, with its wide,
high-resolution stages, dominates cost.  \method{}~\cite{rahman2025effidec3d}
uniformly reduces decoder channels and removes high-resolution stages, preserving
aggregate accuracy across twelve tasks at much lower cost.  These methods answer
\emph{how much} decoder computation can be removed on average; we instead ask
\emph{where} the removed computation would have helped, by comparing a matched
full and efficient decoder rather than proposing a new compression block.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and difficulty estimation.}
Predictive entropy, confidence, and Monte-Carlo dropout~\cite{gal2016dropout} are
widely used to flag ambiguous predictions.  Such signals identify where a model
is uncertain, but ambiguity is not the same as decoder benefit: an uncertain
voxel may remain wrong after additional decoding, and a confident voxel may still
be correctable.  We therefore evaluate signals against held-out \emph{net
transitions} between matched decoders, not against error alone.

\paragraph{Conditional computation and adaptive inference.}
Dynamic networks allocate computation input-adaptively, spatially, or per
sample~\cite{han2021dynamic}.  Realizing such allocation for a 3D decoder
presupposes that the useful computation is spatially concentrated and
identifiable before it is executed---an empirical premise that is usually assumed
rather than tested.  Our study isolates and tests exactly this premise; designing
a trained adaptive decoder is left to future work.

\paragraph{Evaluation and reproducibility.}
Small medical datasets create substantial seed and split variance, and
voxel-level statistical tests can produce severe pseudo-replication.  Our protocol
reports seed-level results, discloses deviations from published baselines, and
bootstraps subjects rather than voxels.  This emphasis on auditing what aggregate
metrics conceal aligns with the goals of the WACV Evaluations \& Datasets track.
